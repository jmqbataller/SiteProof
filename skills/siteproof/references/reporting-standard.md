# Client reporting standard

## Tone
Professional, factual, calm and implementation-oriented. Avoid alarmism, filler, generic AI language, and unsupported certainty.

## Executive summary
State the audit scope, coverage, top business/technical risks, strong areas if meaningful, and the recommended sequence of work. Do not list every low-level observation in the executive summary.

## Finding format
- ID / Category
- Severity
- Verification status
- Finding title
- Affected URL(s)
- Evidence
- Why it matters
- Recommendation
- Owner/next step when known

## Priority plan
**Now**: Critical/High verified defects and business-critical broken journeys.
**Next**: Medium issues and systemic template fixes.
**Later**: Low optimization, hardening and governance improvements.
**Verify**: admin/manual items that cannot be proved publicly.

## Deliverables
- XLSX: working audit inventory with findings, pages, links, evidence.
- DOCX/PDF: client-facing narrative report.
- CSV/JSON: machine-readable handoff/integration.

Never hide scan limitations. A trustworthy audit states what was not tested.
